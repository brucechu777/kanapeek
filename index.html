Kanapeek

Japanese kana and JLPT words on your iPhone Lock Screen. Hiragana and katakana are free; JLPT levels N5 to N1 come with Kanapeek Plus.

Support

Something not working, or a word that looks wrong? Email zuyibo123@gmail.com. We read every message. For a wrong word, tell us the word and what looks wrong (you can copy the address in the app: Settings → Report a wrong word).

Adding the widget to your Lock Screen

  1. Open Kanapeek once and pick your level.
  2. Lock your iPhone, then touch and hold the Lock Screen until Customize appears. Tap Customize, then Lock Screen.
  3. Tap the area under the clock (or the line above it), find Kanapeek in the list, and tap the widget you want.
  4. Tap Done. A new word appears about every hour; iOS decides the exact time of each refresh.

Kanapeek Plus

  • To restore a purchase on a new iPhone: Settings in Kanapeek → Restore purchases.
  • To cancel or change your plan: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Kanapeek Plus. Or in Kanapeek: Settings → Kanapeek Plus → Manage.

Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026

Kanapeek does not collect any data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

  • Your level, your settings, the words you mark as known and your review history are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app and its widget. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Pronunciation uses the Japanese voice built into iOS and runs on your iPhone.
  • If you start the free trial and allow notifications, the reminder before the trial ends is a notification scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details.
  • If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

Kanapeek is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone. If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions: zuyibo123@gmail.com.
