Kanapeek for Android

Japanese words on your Android lock screen. Looking for the iPhone app? See this page.

Support

  • No card on the lock screen? Open Kanapeek and check that "Show cards on lock screen" is on and that "Display over other apps" is allowed. Android only lets an app show a card over the lock screen with that permission.
  • Cards stop after a while? Some phones (for example Samsung or Xiaomi) close background apps to save battery. Set Kanapeek's battery usage to "Unrestricted" in your phone's settings.
  • Too many cards? Settings → Frequency → "Every 2nd time" or "Every 3rd time".
  • Found a wrong word or need help? Email zuyibo123@gmail.com.

Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

Kanapeek has no account and no sign-in. Your level, your settings and the words you mark as learned are stored only on your phone and are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

  • Advertising. Kanapeek shows banner ads inside the app (never on the lock screen card) using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and app interaction and diagnostic data. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Permissions. "Display over other apps" lets Kanapeek open its word card when you turn your screen on. Notifications are used for the small ongoing notification Android requires while Kanapeek waits for the screen to turn on. Neither permission is used to read anything on your phone.
  • Ratings. Kanapeek may ask you to rate it through Google Play's in-app review. Google handles this; we do not see who rated or what they wrote unless it is posted publicly.
  • If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

Kanapeek is not directed at children under 13. If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions: zuyibo123@gmail.com.
